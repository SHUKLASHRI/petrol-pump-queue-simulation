Mathematical Model

1. Poisson Arrivals

Inter-arrival times follow an exponential distribution generated via inverse transform: $\Delta t = -\ln(U) / \lambda$, where $U \sim \text{Uniform}(0,1)$.

2. Service Times

Service duration $S$ is sampled from a Truncated Normal distribution bounded by $[a, b]$ with mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$.

3. Multi-Server Queue (M/G/c, FCFS)

Traffic intensity is $\rho = \lambda / (c \cdot \mu)$, where $\mu = 60 / \bar{S}$ is the hourly service rate per dispenser. The queue is stable when $\rho < 1$ and unstable when $\rho \ge 1$.

4. Little's Law

In steady state, $L_q = \lambda W_q$ and $L = \lambda W$.
